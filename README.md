> *"Precision physics, satisfying rotations, and endless arcade replayability. Time your swipe to flip the soda can and land it upright on dynamic surfaces."*

**FlipThatCan** is a fast-paced physics-based casual arcade game created in Unity with original handcrafted pixel art created in Aseprite. Built around intuitive input feel and momentum calculations, players test their timing to flip beverage cans onto increasingly tricky platforms, tables, and moving obstacles.

## 🎯 Gameplay Features

* **Physics-Driven Can Flips**:
  * Impulse and angular torque equations calculated from touch/drag velocity and release angle.
  * Rigidbody center-of-mass balancing determining whether a landing sticks, wobbles, or rolls off.
* **Upright Landing Detection**:
  * Angle dot-product validation validating that the can base touches down within acceptable pitch/roll tolerances.
* **Combo Multipliers & Streak Scoring**:
  * Multipliers reward consecutive perfect upright landings with dynamic particle confetti and juice effects.
* **Custom Pixel Art & Audio**:
  * Crisp sprite animations crafted in Aseprite paired with crunchy retro arcade sound effects.

---

## 🎮 Controls

* **Mobile**: Tap and swipe upward with varying speed and release timing to control flip height and rotation speed.
* **PC / Web**: Click and drag upward with mouse.
